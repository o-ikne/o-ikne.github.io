Dataset Description

HandsInMotion is a large‑scale, high‑precision motion capture dataset for bimanual hand gesture research. It contains 6,600 sequences (32.6M frames) from 30 performers across 84 gesture classes, recorded with sub‑millimeter MoCap accuracy and integrated into the SMPL‑H body model. Each sequence is paired with both sequence‑ and frame‑level natural language descriptions. Designed as a multi‑task benchmark, it supports text‑to‑motion generation, motion captioning, action‑to‑motion generation, and motion recognition, bridging language and expressive human gesture.
